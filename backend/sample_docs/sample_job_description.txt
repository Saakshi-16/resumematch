Junior AI/ML Engineer - Nimbus Analytics (Bengaluru, Hybrid)

About the role
Nimbus Analytics builds AI assistants that help insurance and banking companies answer customer questions from their documents. We are hiring a Junior AI/ML Engineer to join our GenAI team and help build Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) applications used by thousands of customers.

Responsibilities
- Build and improve RAG pipelines: document parsing, chunking, embeddings and retrieval.
- Integrate large language models (LLMs) such as GPT, Gemini or Llama into production services.
- Develop backend REST APIs in Python (Django or FastAPI).
- Store and search embeddings using vector databases such as FAISS, Pinecone or ChromaDB.
- Evaluate model quality using metrics and test datasets, and improve accuracy over time.
- Containerize services with Docker and deploy them on AWS (EC2, S3, Lambda).
- Collaborate with product managers and explain technical results clearly to non-technical teams.

Required qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering or a related field (2025 or 2026 graduates welcome).
- Strong programming skills in Python.
- Solid understanding of machine learning fundamentals and NLP.
- Hands-on experience with at least one deep learning framework such as PyTorch or TensorFlow.
- Experience building REST APIs.
- Working knowledge of SQL and relational databases.
- Familiarity with Git and collaborative development.
- Experience with Docker.
- Good communication and teamwork skills.

Preferred qualifications (nice to have)
- Hands-on experience with LLMs, prompt engineering or RAG.
- Experience with vector databases (FAISS, Pinecone, ChromaDB).
- Experience deploying applications on AWS or another cloud platform.
- Knowledge of Kubernetes or MLOps tools such as MLflow.
- Basic frontend skills in React.

What we offer
Mentorship from senior engineers, a learning budget, flexible hybrid work and the chance to ship AI products used by real customers.
